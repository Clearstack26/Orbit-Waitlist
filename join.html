Record a quick voice note At the event or when you get home, record for thirty seconds. Say their name, role, and what you talked about. Orbit builds their profile from your voice. Already scanned their card? Add the note straight onto their profile.
03
Every contact, tagged to the event Orbit organises everyone you met by event. When you save a note, choose which event it belongs to. Find anyone from any event in seconds. No more scrolling through notes, no more guessing who was who.
04
Orbit keeps you accountable When you create an event, set your goal. Want to follow up with 10 people? Orbit tracks every one.
